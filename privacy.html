Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this portfolio website ("the Website") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit.

1. Information Collected

  • Contact form: your name, email address, subject, and message — used only to respond to your inquiry.
  • Theme preference: your dark/light mode choice is stored locally in your browser (localStorage). It never leaves your device.
  • Server logs: GitHub Pages may collect standard, anonymous traffic logs.

2. How Information Is Used

Messages sent through the contact form are forwarded to the site owner's email inbox solely for the purpose of replying to you. Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing.

3. Third-Party Services

The Website relies on these providers, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages — static site hosting
  • Formspree — contact form delivery (when enabled)
  • cdnjs / Google Fonts — stylesheet, icon, and font delivery

4. Cookies & Tracking

This Website does not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or advertising. The only stored value is your theme preference in localStorage.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any information you have submitted by emailing abdulshooter4303m@email.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
